A full-time lecturer is allocated 40% teaching, 40% research and 20% service in the annual workload model.

Teaching a new subject for the first time counts as 1.5 times the normal teaching load.

Workload allocations for the next year are confirmed by the Head of School in November.

Lecturers who supervise more than four honours students receive a reduction of one subject in the following session.
